Our story

Consistency is the hard part. So we built around it.

Most problems in AI training data come from inconsistency, not from a lack of effort. Labels drift when guidelines are vague, when teams change and when nobody checks the work systematically.

{{ brand.name }} is organised around preventing that. Every project has a dedicated team with clear roles. Every annotator is trained and tested on the project guideline before production. Every batch passes through review and QA — and what QA finds goes back to the team as feedback the next day.

Our skilled, remote annotation workforce works across image, video and text annotation, with a particular focus on human action and video understanding. We support projects from short pilots to large, long-running datasets.

We aim for long-term collaboration: the longer a team works on your data, the better it understands your edge cases — and the more consistent your dataset becomes.
